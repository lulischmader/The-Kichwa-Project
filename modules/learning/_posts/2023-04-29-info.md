## Learning
The Kichwa Project offers free and accessible learning resources for anyone interested in the Kichwa language. The materials are designed to support learners at all levels and include:

- Kichwa language lessons and modules  
- Audio and video materials  
- Interactive exercises and quizzes  
- Vocabulary lists and flashcards  
- Grammar guides and explanations  

All resources are available online at any time. Each course is self-paced, allowing you to learn according to your own schedule and at the speed that works best for you.
