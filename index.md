# Kichwata Yachakushun
## Aprendamos Kichwa
## Let's learn Kichwa

![Photo logo](/images/Photo_logo.png){: style="border-radius:50%; width: 225px;"}

Kichwa is a variety of the Quechua language spoken by Indigenous Peoples of the Andes, primarily in Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, and Colombia. This project aims to promote the Kichwa language and culture by offering free, accessible online resources that support self-guided learning. By gathering texts, videos, and other relevant materials in one place, the project makes it easier for learners to find everything they need in a single, organized space.
